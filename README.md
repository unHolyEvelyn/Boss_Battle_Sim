# Boss_Battle_Sim
Boss Battle Simulator game, where 3 classes of heroes fight a powerful boss with differing strategies.

Featuring 3 classes of hero:
- **Fighter:** Balanced offense and Defense.  Can stack his attacks, for a price.
- **Mage:** Glass Canon that needs to combo detonation spells with instant spells for massive waves of damage.  Self Healing.
- **Tank:** Low offense, high defense.  Perfectly parry Boss attacks to negate damage, and counter strike with your own!
